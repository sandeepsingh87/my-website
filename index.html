About Me

Building Quality at Scale

Testing strategist who turns complexity into reliable software.

Hi, I'm Sandeep Singh — a Manager of End-to-End Testing with over 16 years of experience in software testing and quality assurance, primarily in the financial services sector.

In my current role at Western Union, I lead and manage a team of testers, develop and implement testing strategies, and ensure the quality of software products that serve millions of customers globally.

I'm passionate about the intersection of AI and Quality Engineering — exploring how automation, machine learning, and intelligent test frameworks can elevate software reliability. I believe quality is not just a phase; it's a culture.

Outside work, I follow football and cricket, read on technology and finance, and enjoy sharing knowledge through mentoring and community engagement.
